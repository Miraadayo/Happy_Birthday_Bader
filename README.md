# A Gift for Bader 🎁

A small interactive birthday gift, made by **Mira** for **Bader**'s 24th birthday.
It is a single web page that plays like a short story: a game with Asuka, a letter in a bottle, a star game of memories, and a closing page.

Best viewed on a phone in landscape mode.

---

## The Journey

### 1. Title screen
A welcome screen with the words *Happy Birthday, Bader* and a **Start Game** button.

### 2. Asuka's game
A short visual-novel style game where Asuka Langley Soryu appears to give Bader a birthday gift, and of course she pretends she doesn't care.

- **Three chapters:** *Late Again*, *Dreams*, and *The Gift*.
- **Choices:** Bader answers Asuka's questions, such as what he dreams about and whether he deserves the gift. Each choice earns points and changes how Asuka reacts.
- **Hearts:** a mood meter that fills up as Asuka warms up to him.
- **Three endings:** good, medium, and bad, depending on the points collected.
- **Japanese voice:** every line is spoken in Japanese, with the English text shown on screen. There are 26 recorded voice lines, and a button to turn the voice on or off.
- **Scenery and sound effects:** each chapter has its own background (sunset, night, a warm room, a party) with floating details, and sound effects for angry, happy, and shy reactions, hearts, chapter cards, and endings.

### 3. A message in a bottle
After the game, a glass bottle floats on a calm anime-style sea. Tapping it pops the cork, and Mira's letter rises out of the bottle. The letter is written letter by letter, as if by magic, and the screen follows the writing.
Soft rain and a song play during this part and continue until the end of the site.

### 4. Our Memories
A star game. Six stars float in the sky, and each one reveals a word and a memory between Mira and Bader:

1. **Where it began**: how they first met in a MOBA game.
2. **Our games**: Mobile Legends and Minecraft together.
3. **The way you treat me**: the laughs and the late nights.
4. **When I fell for you**: how it turned into love.
5. **My safe place**: feeling safe and protective.
6. **My whole world**: what he means to her.

Collecting all six stars opens the way to the last page.

### 5. The end
A closing page with a final message, **Made By mira**, and a funny wobbling *Blblblbl*. There is also a **Play again** button to start the whole journey over.

---

## Sound

- Sound effects are generated by the browser, with no extra files.
- The Asuka voice lines are recorded clips stored inside the page.
- The music and the rain only play from the letter page onward, never during the game.

## Made With

Plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript in a single file. No libraries, no frameworks.
